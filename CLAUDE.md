# CLAUDE.md

## Purpose
This repository contains the website for an independent, off-campus student matchmaking event for Northeastern students.

The site exists to explain the concept clearly, build trust quickly, and drive completed applications.

## Project priorities
Prioritize, in this order:
1. clarity of the offer
2. trustworthiness
3. mobile usability
4. speed of iteration
5. visual polish

Do not trade clarity for cleverness.
Do not add complexity unless it materially improves conversion or usability.

## Audience
The audience is college students, primarily Northeastern students between roughly 18 and 24.

Assume visitors:
- are mobile-first
- have short attention spans
- are sensitive to anything that feels fake, corporate, scammy, or overdesigned
- respond better to directness than brand theater

## Product understanding
This is a student-run matchmaking event, not a dating app.

Core product facts:
- users answer a short questionnaire
- matching is reviewed manually
- selected participants are invited to an off-campus event
- attendance is paid
- the reveal happens in person

When making product or copy decisions, preserve those facts and do not obscure them.

## UX principles
Every page should help a visitor quickly understand:
- what this is
- who it is for
- how it works
- whether it costs money
- what to do next

Prefer simple page flows.
Prefer fewer sections.
Prefer one strong CTA over many competing actions.

If there is a tradeoff between expression and comprehension, choose comprehension.

## Content principles
Copy should be:
- concise
- specific
- natural-sounding
- socially aware
- easy to skim

Avoid:
- startup jargon
- luxury brand language
- vague poetic copy
- long defensive explanations
- filler sections
- fake urgency
- fake testimonials

Do not hide material information such as pricing or event format.

## Implementation principles
Build the simplest thing that can credibly ship.

Prefer:
- maintainable structure
- low complexity
- lightweight interactions
- strong defaults
- embedded flows over fragmented flows when appropriate

Avoid unnecessary abstractions, animation systems, or architectural complexity for MVP work.

## Design collaboration
For all UI and visual decisions, also consult `DESIGN.md`.

`CLAUDE.md` defines product context, priorities, and working principles.
`DESIGN.md` defines the visual system and aesthetic constraints.

If the two ever seem to conflict, optimize for user trust, clarity, and project goals.

## Decision rules
When uncertain:
- choose the clearer headline
- choose the shorter section
- choose the more honest explanation
- choose the simpler interaction
- choose the version that feels more student-native and less corporate

## Scope control
This project should stay lean.
Do not invent extra pages, fake social proof, dashboards, accounts, or complex flows unless explicitly requested.

Default to a concise landing page with a clear application path.

## Working style
Make small, high-leverage changes.
Do not rewrite everything unless necessary.
Preserve what is already working.
Explain tradeoffs clearly when proposing changes.
