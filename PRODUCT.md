# Product Strategy: Matchmaking

Strategic context for the Northeastern Student Matchmaking landing page.

## Register
**Brand surface** (marketing landing page for a student-run social matchmaking event). Spacing, layout, and visual character are primary drivers of engagement.

## Audience
- **Who**: Northeastern University undergraduate students, mostly ages 18 to 24.
- **Characteristics**: Fast-scrolling, mobile-first, socially tuned-in.
- **Pain Point**: Tired of low-effort, algorithm-driven dating apps and forced corporate branding.
- **Mental Model**: Seeking a human-curated, warm, low-pressure, and authentic campus event.

## Brand Voice
- **Warm**: Friendly, inviting, conversational, and peer-to-peer.
- **Intentional**: Honest, transparent about how matching works, no marketing fluff.
- **Authentic**: Student-made, lightly playful, avoiding corporate or clinical tones.

## Visual References
- Cozy off-campus student lounges with warm lighting and plants.
- Physical print flyers and polaroids.
- Lightweight, editorial serif typography paired with clean, premium sans-serif details.

## Anti-References
- **Corporate Tech-Bro SaaS**: Monochromatic blue landing pages, feature grids, abstract vector illustrations, "optimize your connection" jargon.
- **Dating App Clichés**: Swiping layouts, cheesy hearts, roses, pink gradients.
- **Fake Urgency**: Countdown timers, fake registration counters, artificial scarcity.
